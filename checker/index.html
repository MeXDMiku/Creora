What can a stranger read from your database?

Your app has rules about who can see what. This asks your live backend whether they actually hold — using only the key your site already ships to every visitor.

Read-only. No writes, no deletes, no service key. Everything runs in your own browser — nothing is sent to us, and you can watch it in your network tab. It does only what any visitor’s browser can already do.

The key that starts sb_publishable_ or eyJ in your frontend is the safe one. Never paste a service_role or sb_secret_ key — this refuses it, but it should never leave your machine anyway.

Runs entirely in your browser. Open the page source — there is no analytics, no tracking, and no server but yours.
